Case Studies / Case study

Feeling Good

As Founding Designer at Feeling Good, I transformed cognitive-behavioral therapy principles into a user-friendly app, leading design from inception to launch. My work centered on intuitive UI/UX to enhance mental wellness, ensuring the app was accessible and engaging for users seeking to improve their mental health.

Client
Feeling Good Inc
Role
Founding Designer
Year
2023
Services
Brand, Mobile, Product

Original deck on Pitch

01 / Overview

Therapy techniques, in an app

Feeling Good is a mobile platform for Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) techniques. It helps people combat negative thoughts through evidence-based lessons, and it draws on the pioneering work of Dr. David Burns and his book Feeling Great.

As Founding Designer I owned the design strategy and the product vision: user experience, interface design and product management. I led all design work, ran the user research, hired and mentored the design team, and managed the product from concept to launch.

What I did

  1. 01Owned the app's core user flows: onboarding and the primary daily-use experience.
  2. 02Built the web and mobile design system from scratch: component libraries, tokens and governance documentation.
  3. 03Ran 30+ user interviews and used the findings to reshape the interface.
  4. 04Worked with licensed psychologists to align the product with clinical requirements.

02 / Problem

Mental health is a real problem

Feeling Good set out to make mental health accessible. These are the numbers the project started from.

03 / Goals

What the business needed

The company had three business goals for the app: raise funding, build a revenue model, and keep the people who sign up.

04 / Research

Getting to the source: people

I ran in-depth interviews with over 30 potential users to understand their main challenges, their lifestyle, and the positive impact the book Feeling Great had on them.

The aim was to find the core user needs and work out how the book's principles could be adapted into an accessible mobile app. These insights were pivotal in shaping an app that supports its users' mental health journey.

05 / Measuring

Quantifying success

To evaluate the impact of the CBT exercises on users' mental well-being, we used a Likert scale to assess shifts in emotional states: the increase in positive feelings and the decrease in negative feelings, before and after users complete a CBT session.

Gathering this data let us quantify the therapeutic benefit of the exercises, and keep improving the app's content to better serve users' mental health needs.

06 / Interface

More than just education

The old user interface was clunky, confusing and repetitive, and it did not stir delight. We also needed a different approach than educational content alone: a chat interface that can quickly get a user to a place where they are feeling good.

07 / Learning

What inspired us

To improve the app's educational experience, we modeled our approach on Duolingo's proven method. Their efficient and engaging system inspired our streamlined learning paths for CBT courses, designed to maintain user motivation and facilitate effective learning.

08 / Delight

Delight, carefully

After refining the branding, the user research and the UI designs, we moved to the next phase of enhancing the app's impact. The goal was to infuse elements of joy into the lives of users dealing with depression and anxiety, carefully balancing these features so they uplift rather than overwhelm.

09 / Results

Results

Funding exceeded its goal, more than 1,000 users joined the waitlist, and 90% improved feelings.
