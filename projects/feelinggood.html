Feeling Good

As Founding Designer at Feeling Good, I transformed cognitive-behavioral therapy principles into a user-friendly app, leading design from inception to launch. My work centered on intuitive UI/UX to enhance mental wellness, ensuring the app was accessible and engaging for users seeking to improve their mental health.

Client
Feeling Good Inc
Role
Founding Designer
Year
2023
Services
Brand, Mobile, Product

Full case study deck

What I did:

  1. 01Owned the app's core user flows: onboarding and the primary daily-use experience.
  2. 02Built the web and mobile design system from scratch: component libraries, tokens and governance documentation.
  3. 03Ran 30+ user interviews and used the findings to reshape the interface.
  4. 04Worked with licensed psychologists to align the product with clinical requirements.
